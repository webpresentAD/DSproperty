Privacy & Cookie Policy

This policy explains how DS Property Renovation Ltd handles personal information in connection with this website. Last updated: 21 October 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by DS Property Renovation Ltd. For any question about privacy or your personal information, email us at dsproperty.uk@outlook.com or call +44 7955 952660.

Information we collect

This website has no contact form, no user accounts, and no analytics or advertising tools. We do not collect personal information through the pages of the website itself.

If you choose to phone or email us, we receive the information you decide to share, such as your name, contact details and project details. We use it to respond to your enquiry, provide a quote and, if you go ahead, carry out the work we have discussed. We keep it only for as long as we reasonably need it for those purposes.

Phone and email links

The phone and email links on this website open the phone or email app on your device. Any call or message is then handled by your phone provider or email service under their own terms and privacy policies, not through this website.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not set cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage to save a single item, your cookie choice, so that we do not ask you again on every visit. This item stays on your device and is not sent to us.

No analytics, advertising or other tracking technologies are currently used on this website. Your choice is recorded, but because no non-essential tools are in use it does not currently change how the site works. You can review your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer, or remove it by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Links to other websites

This website links to other websites, such as Donda Digital, which is credited in the footer. Those websites are responsible for their own privacy practices, and this policy does not cover them.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Contact us using the details above. If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.
